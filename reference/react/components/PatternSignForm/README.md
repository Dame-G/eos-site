Signing forms: a list of what is outstanding, then one form to read and sign.

- **Built from:** Notice, ItemList and ItemRow, Surface, BackLink, SignatureField (`mode="both"`), FileUpload, Actions.
- The list says how many need action and by when; each row has one Button, "Review and sign", and a Signed row offers a download instead.
- The signature step explains what signing means in a sentence, offers typing or drawing, and records the date. The typed option is always there for people who cannot draw.
- Submit stays one clear Button; saving for later is the quieter choice beside it.
