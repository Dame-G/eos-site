A person, as a photo or as their initials on a rounded tile (the same shape as the date on an AppointmentCard). Props: `name` (read by screen readers), `src` (a photo URL; if it fails to load the initials appear), `small` (28px, for rows) and `large` (56px, for a practitioner on an AppointmentCard or a profile). The photo is cropped to a circle and is always decorative beside the name, so give it no alt text of its own. Show a photo only where the practice or the person chose to supply one.
