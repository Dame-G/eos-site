A group of Buttons. The rule for every group in the system: they sit in the order of the content, the main action first, left-aligned, and wrap when there is no room. Props: the Buttons as children, `bar` (puts the group on a tinted footer bar with a rule above), `as` (use footer for a bar at the end of a card) and `className`. On a phone, a bar's Buttons stack full width.

**Bar or inline.** A group that ends a card or an overlay is a bar: Dialog, Drawer, Popover, StatementCard, StatementRow, AppointmentCard, and any Surface given `actions`. A group in the flow of a page or a message is inline: a form on the page, a Notice, an EmptyState, a PageHeader, the Today and Clear row of a Calendar. The test is what the group ends. A card's actions are about that card, so they sit on its foot; a page's actions are the next step in a flow.
