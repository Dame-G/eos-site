One statement as a flat card (it is not clickable; its Buttons are): StatusStamp, AmountFigure, a one-sentence reason, label and text parts, a History disclosure, then Actions. Props: `title`, `subtitle`, `status` (or `tone` and `label`), `detail` (a due date), `amount`, `amountLabel`, `details` (lines under the amount), `reason`, `parts` (`{label, text}`: what it covers, how to pay, when it is due), `activity` with `activityTitle`, `actions` (Pay now first, then Download), `currency`, `locale`, `headingLevel`.

- The reason comes first: what this is and what to do, in plain words.
- One lead amount, up to three parts, history behind the disclosure.
- Lay statements out in a Grid, most urgent first. A status is never folded into a score.
